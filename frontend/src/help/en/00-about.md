# What is transkribierBAR?

transkribierBAR turns speech in audio and video files into text. You upload a file or record directly in the browser. You correct the text in the browser while the recording plays, and download it as subtitles (SRT, VTT), as plain text (TXT) or as JSON. On request, a language model summarizes the content, writes minutes, refines the text or translates it.

## What you can use transkribierBAR for

- **Research interviews:** transcribe interviews with each speaker's contributions kept apart, then give each speaker a name.
- **Meetings:** record a meeting and turn it into minutes or notes that follow your agenda.
- **Teaching:** create subtitles for lecture and teaching videos, correct them and translate them into other languages.

## Access

Sign in at **transcription.uni-osnabrueck.de** with your Uni-Account. You then see the overview of your transcriptions. The interface is available in German and English.

## Data protection and consent

> **Your data stays at the university.** transkribierBAR runs on servers of Osnabrück University, and so do its speech recognition and its AI features. Your files and texts are not passed on to external providers. In the app, only you can see your transcriptions. For more, see the [privacy notice](/handbuch/en/privacy/).

> **Get consent first.** Only record conversations, and only upload recordings, if everyone who speaks in them has agreed.
